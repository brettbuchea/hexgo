HexGo Edge Eyes rule set: single eyes and reference cases

Edge areas count as eyes. Each group is assumed to have no liberties outside its wall, so only its eyes keep it alive. "Alive" means the attacker can't capture it even with unlimited free moves; "Dead" means the attacker kills it even when the defender replies. One example per type is shown: the one with the fewest wall stones.
