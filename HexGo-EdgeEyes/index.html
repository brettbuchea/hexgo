Rules in brief

Liberty pair: two empty cells that touch each other and both touch the group. A lone stone with enemies on every other side is captured.

Edges: walking round an edge stone, skip the off-board cells; the cells either side of the gap count as next to each other, so liberties left and right along the edge make a pair.

Eyes: empty cells the group fully rings with its own stones. An area that touches the board edge is never an eye (it still counts as territory). Two or more eye cells anywhere keep it alive; exactly two is "atari".

Order: the opponent's dead groups are removed first, so filling a two-point eye captures even though the stone looks like suicide. Pure suicide and repeated positions are illegal.

Dashed red ring = group the opponent can capture in one move.
